IMU Nav is a research-prototype navigator for Ukraine that continues estimating progress along a planned route when GPS is jammed, spoofed, or otherwise untrusted.

Every GPS fix is classified as good, suspect, or bad. When trusted GPS is unavailable, the app follows the selected route using phone motion sensors, cell-tower positioning, road data, and map knowledge. Car and walking modes use separate motion models.

Features include:

* offline car and walking routes using user-installed GraphHopper packs
* offline address search when the installed pack contains a search index
* MapLibre maps with downloadable or importable offline map data
* cell-tower positioning from a bundled database or user-imported data
* trip history, recording, recovery, replay, and map matching
* optional online OSRM routing and Photon search fallback
* English, Ukrainian, and Russian interfaces with optional spoken directions

The app has no advertising, analytics, crash reporting, Google Play Services, or Firebase. Optional online routing and search can be disabled, and supported servers can be self-hosted or configured in the app.

IMU Nav is not road-tested safety equipment. Treat it as a research prototype and never rely on it as a safety system.
