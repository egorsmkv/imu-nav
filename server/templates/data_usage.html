What is stored

An account stores your email, a password hash, session metadata, and consent receipts. Tower sharing stores a random device ID, cell identifiers, estimated tower coordinates, range, sample count and update time. The server uses an IP address in memory for rate limits but does not store it as a device ID or in application request logs.

Optional developer diagnostics contain trip events, precise location, sensor readings, log lines, device and app context. The server excludes passwords and tokens. Administrators can review private diagnostics, tower observations, import errors and audit records.

Purpose, sharing and retention

Public downloads contain combined tower positions and removals, without email addresses or device IDs. Accepted account tower observations expire after 12 calendar months; the server then recomputes consensus. Diagnostics expire after 30 days. Expired authentication records and old import errors are removed during daily cleanup. Admin audit records expire after 90 days. Operator backups and reverse proxy logs have separate controls.

Tower uploads and precise-location diagnostics require separate, versioned consent. An updated notice requires fresh consent. Withdrawing tower consent deletes that account's observations and recomputes shared positions. Withdrawing diagnostics consent deletes that account's diagnostic sessions. Another device's observations or seed data can still support a tower; downloaded copies and preexisting exports cannot be recalled.

Your controls

Your account lets you review observations, download a compressed NDJSON archive of your account data, delete observations and close the account. Your diagnostics lets you review and delete sessions. Use the app Settings to grant or withdraw each upload purpose. Contact the operator above for other requests. Account closure removes account data from the live database; bounded backups may retain a copy until expiry.
